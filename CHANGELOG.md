# Changelog

## 0.1.4 (2026-11-05)

- The signs on the yard ladder switches face the right way, as on the main line switches.

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-04)

- The offices' kiosks work with Career Manager API: no more error at load, and Railroad Contracts' menu shows there too.

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-03)

- The yards now show in the distant view, as stock yards do.
- The tunnels' concrete no longer shows a tiled pattern of portal pictures in some games.
- Works with Double Tracked Valley and DV Signals. Start a new career with Double Tracked Valley: added to an existing one, it removes every car you don't own.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-03)

- Unity Mod Manager now checks for new versions and can install them.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-03)

First release.

- Two interchange yards on the stock map: the West Interchange (WI) beside the Machine Factory, and the Northeast Interchange (NEI) on the plateau west of Iron Ore Mine East, each with a line into a tunnel off the map.
- An office at each yard with a career manager kiosk, a job validator and a booklet table.
- Both yards on the game's maps, with fast travel to their offices, and in the station booklet and route map.
