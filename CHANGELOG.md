## 0.1.1 (2026-11-03)

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-03)

First release.

- Two interchange yards on the stock map: the West Interchange (WI) beside the Machine Factory, and the Northeast Interchange (NEI) on the plateau west of Iron Ore Mine East, each with a line into a tunnel off the map.
- An office at each yard with a career manager kiosk, a job validator and a booklet table.
- Both yards on the game's maps, with fast travel to their offices, and in the station booklet and route map.
